# Abilene Weekend Events Flyer

The shareable weekend events flyer for Abilene, Texas: the week's events, shows, and pop-ups across the 30 to 60 minute ring around town.

**Live:** [weekend.northbrand.net](https://weekend.northbrand.net) (Cloudflare Pages, project `abileneweekend`; also answers at abileneweekend.pages.dev)

## Refresh

- **Wednesdays**: the full events digest rebuilds the flyer lineup and republishes it
- **Fridays, about 12:00**: a weather-only refresh republishes the same flyer with the weekend forecast updated

Each publish pushes the page here as the archive of record, uploads it to Cloudflare Pages, and verifies the live page.

## What gets in

Family friendly events, theater and stage shows, comedians, major bands and concerts, art and photography, antique and vintage finds, maker fairs, and notable experiences. Left out: loud dive bars and club nights, and religious events of any kind.

The flyer is a public page: it never carries family names or private calendar details.

## Design

Burnt orange on warm paper, inside the Northbrand shell (Northbrand Gold accent, serif display, the footer lockup). The shared design DNA lives in the northbrand-hub repo.
